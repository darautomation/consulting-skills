Two plugins

call-copilot

Any consulting or helping profession
  • Pre-call brief from calendar, chat, email, public profiles
  • Transcription: Zoom, any audio, VTT/SRT; Whisper hallucinations filtered
  • Client recap + CRM note, kept separate
  • Pre-send guard: right person, no placeholders, no AI tells
  • Presets: coach, therapist, realtor, lawyer, advisor, consultant

advisor-copilot

Financial advisors, planners, wealth managers
  • Discovery brief with money snapshot and residency
  • Recap with confirmed money facts and compliance notes
  • Plan draft: goals, cashflow, three buckets, asset location
  • 13-meeting service year, one outcome per meeting
  • Annual review template

Rules that came from real mistakes

Wrong-person guard

A recap once went to the wrong client. Now every message must contain the recipient's name and no one else's.

Notes over AI rewrite

The client saw the meeting notes being written. They get those, nearly verbatim. Analysis stays internal.

Nothing sent without "yes"

The skill drafts. You approve. Silence is never consent.

No invented facts

Gaps become "ask on the call". A profile counts only when two signals match.

Privacy by niche

Therapy and legal presets keep sensitive content out of messengers. Local Whisper supported.

Short beats complete

You read the brief between two meetings. The point is in the first two lines.
