# Stage 5. Results: progress across sessions

Goal: the consultant sees where the client is against the goal and walks into the next session with a 3-item agenda.

## Inputs

All CRM notes / recaps for this client (stage 3 outputs), the original goal (first-call note or contract), payments or package status if the user tracks them.

## Report

```
{Client} · session {done}/{planned} · since {start date}

GOAL: {what the client came for, in their words}
MOVED: what changed since the start, with dates and numbers from notes
PROMISED vs DONE:
  us:     ✅ / ⏳ / ❌ per item
  client: ✅ / ⏳ / ❌ per item
STUCK: items open > 14 days, repeated topics, avoided topics
RELATIONSHIP RISKS: silence > 14 days, missed sessions, payment overdue, tone change
NEXT SESSION AGENDA (3 items max): ...
SUGGESTED MESSAGE (optional, draft only): a short check-in if the client went quiet
```

## Rules

- Every "MOVED" line cites the note date it comes from. No invented progress.
- Outcome metrics come from the niche preset (sessions completed, plan delivered, offer accepted, deal closed, symptom scale, savings rate).
- The check-in message is a draft. It follows stage 4 approval rules.
- Testimonials and case studies: suggest asking only after a visible result, and only with written permission; never publish client details.
