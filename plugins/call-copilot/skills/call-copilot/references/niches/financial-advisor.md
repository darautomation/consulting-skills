# Preset: financial advisor, wealth manager, financial planner

For the full advisor workflow (discovery, goals and cashflow, risk profile, plan building, annual service cadence) install the **advisor-copilot** plugin from the same marketplace. This preset covers the basics.

- **Call types**: discovery, plan presentation, implementation, regular review, annual review.
- **Pre-call brief extras**: goals with amounts and dates, income/expenses if known, assets and liabilities, current products, risk attitude, jurisdiction and tax residency, products discussed earlier.
- **Discovery questions**: What should this money do for you and by when? What would be a bad year for you, in numbers? Where is your income coming from in 10 years? What do you already own and why? Who else decides?
- **Client recap**: goals as agreed, numbers the client confirmed, documents to send, next meeting.
- **Outcome metrics**: plan delivered, implementation done, contributions on schedule, review meetings held.
- **Compliance**: no return promises; recap is not investment advice unless you are licensed for it in the client's jurisdiction; add your disclaimer from config.
