# Preset: lawyer, accountant, tax advisor

- **Call types**: initial consultation, case/engagement update, filing review.
- **Pre-call brief extras**: matter or engagement, deadlines, documents received and missing, conflict check status (ask the professional, never assume).
- **Discovery questions**: What outcome do you need and by when? What documents do you have? Who else is involved? What has been done so far?
- **Client recap**: facts as stated, documents requested with deadline, next steps and who owns them, fee or engagement status. No legal or tax opinion that the professional did not give explicitly.
- **Outcome metrics**: documents collected, deadlines met, engagement signed.
- **Confidentiality**: send recaps only through the channel set in the engagement letter; see `../privacy-consent.md`.
