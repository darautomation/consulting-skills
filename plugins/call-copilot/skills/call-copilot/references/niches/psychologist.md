# Preset: psychologist, therapist, counsellor

Highest confidentiality level. Read `../privacy-consent.md` first.

- **Call types**: intake, regular session, review every 6-8 sessions, closing.
- **Pre-call brief**: logistics, previous session's agreed homework, client's own stated goals, risk notes the therapist explicitly marked. No web search of the client by default: ask the therapist before searching anything public.
- **Intake questions**: What brings you here now? What would be different if therapy helped? What support do you have? Previous therapy experience? Any safety concerns (follow the therapist's protocol, never improvise crisis guidance).
- **Client recap**: logistics + exercise or homework the client agreed to + next session time. Never diagnoses, symptoms, trauma content or third-party details in a messenger or email.
- **Internal note**: SOAP or DAP format if the therapist uses one; otherwise themes, interventions used, client response, plan.
- **Outcome metrics**: sessions attended, self-reported scale the therapist uses (e.g., 0-10 distress), homework completion.
- **Red flags**: any mention of self-harm or harm to others → flag at the top of the internal note for the therapist; do not write anything to the client about it.
- **Address**: as the therapist addresses the client (config).
