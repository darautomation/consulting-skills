# Preset: consultant (default)

Agencies, B2B consultants, mentors, private-practice specialists, anyone selling expertise through calls.

- **Call types**: discovery (first), working session, review, renewal.
- **Discovery questions**: What made you book now? What have you tried already, and what happened? What does success look like in 90 days, in numbers? Who else decides? What happens if nothing changes?
- **Core block of the call plan**: diagnose → show 1 quick win live → outline the path.
- **Typical objections**: "need to think" → agree on what exactly and a date; "too expensive" → compare to the cost of the problem per month; "no time" → show the smallest first step.
- **Outcome metrics**: next session booked, proposal accepted, project KPI moved.
- **Address**: first name, informal unless config says formal.
