# Preset: realtor, property consultant, relocation advisor

- **Call types**: buyer/seller consultation, property review, offer strategy, closing coordination.
- **Pre-call brief extras**: budget, financing status (pre-approved or not), areas, must-haves, timeline, properties already sent and reactions, decision makers.
- **Discovery questions**: Why move now? Budget including closing costs? Financing ready? Three must-haves and three deal-breakers? When must you be in?
- **Client recap**: shortlist with links, viewings booked, documents the client must send, next decision date.
- **Outcome metrics**: viewings done, offers made, offer accepted, days to close.
- **Objections**: "prices will drop" → show local data you have, not predictions; "another agent offered less commission" → what the client gets for the difference.
- **Compliance**: no promises on price growth or rental yield; no discriminatory wording about neighbourhoods.
