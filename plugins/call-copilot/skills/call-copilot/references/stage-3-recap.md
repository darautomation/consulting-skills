# Stage 3. Recap: transcript/notes → client recap + CRM note

Two different outputs for two different readers. Never mix them.

## Source priority

| Source | Client recap | Internal CRM note |
|---|---|---|
| Platform notes (`notes.txt`) | **yes, nearly verbatim** | yes |
| Long platform summary (`ai_summary.txt`) | no | yes |
| Raw transcript | only as a labelled DRAFT when no notes exist | yes, main source for details |

Why: the client saw the notes being written during the call and agreed with them. A fresh AI rewrite introduces new wording, new emphasis and sometimes new facts. That is where complaints come from.

**No notes yet?** If the call ended less than ~2 hours ago, platform notes may still be processing. Say "notes not ready, recap queued" instead of writing one from the transcript.

## Client recap

Format (adapt headings to the niche preset):

```
{DD.MM} · {session N if the niche counts sessions} · {topic}

{platform notes, cleaned: remove [Quote] markers, footnote numbers, timestamps, speaker IDs; keep their structure}

Next steps:            ← only if the notes have no next-steps section of their own
1. {who} {does what} {by when}

Recording: {link, only if the consultant shares recordings}
```

Cleaning allowed: formatting, obvious mis-hearings of names and terms, removing internal remarks ("I'll check with my partner about the commission"). Not allowed: rewriting, adding advice that was not given, softening or strengthening what was said.

## Internal CRM note

```
{date} · {client} · {call type: first / regular / review}
Context: 1-2 lines
What we learned: facts, numbers, decisions (with who said it)
Client's words: 1-3 short quotes that show motivation or worry
Promised by us: ...           ← feeds stage 4
Promised by client: ...
Risks / red flags: ...
Next session: date if agreed, agenda draft
```

Extract every line where the consultant says "I'll send / prepare / check / introduce". These are commitments. List them separately under "Promised by us" and ask the consultant which ones to fulfil in stage 4: never send materials on your own.

## Group calls and webinars

Skip the personal recap. Produce one general summary (key points, Q&A, links mentioned) and ask the consultant who should receive it.
