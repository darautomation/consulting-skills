# Recap and CRM note after a client meeting

Follow call-copilot stage 3. Finance-specific additions:

**Client recap**: platform notes cleaned + "Next steps" with documents to send and deadlines + meeting number if in service year. Numbers only as the client confirmed them in the call.

**Internal note** adds:
```
Money facts learned: (amount, currency, period, source: "said in call" / "document")
Goals changed: ...
Products discussed: name, why, client reaction (no recommendation is final until advisor confirms)
Compliance: suitability notes, risk profile changes, disclosures given
Meeting # in service year: N, outcome done / not
```

Commitments: every "I'll send you / prepare / calculate" by the advisor → list, ask the advisor which to fulfil. Never send product materials on your own.
