# Discovery and meeting prep

## Brief before the meeting

Same format as call-copilot stage 1 (who, history, goal, plan, questions, objections, next step), plus:

```
MONEY SNAPSHOT (only confirmed): income/month, expenses/month, surplus, liquid assets, property, debts, currencies
GOALS: goal · amount · date · priority
RESIDENCY: tax residency, citizenship(s), planned moves
CURRENT PRODUCTS: what they hold, where, why they bought it
GAPS: what we still need for a plan draft
```

## Discovery meeting plan (60 min)

| Min | Block | Aim |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 | Rapport, agenda | client talks first |
| 5-20 | Life and goals | what money is for, dates, people involved |
| 20-35 | Money picture | income, expenses, assets, debts, what worries them |
| 35-45 | Risk and experience | worst year they lived through, what they did |
| 45-55 | How we work | process, service year, fees, what they get |
| 55-60 | Next step | documents to send, date of plan presentation |

## Questions that work

- What should this money do for you, and by when?
- If everything goes well, what does your life look like in 10 years? Where do you live?
- What would a bad year look like for you in numbers? What did you do in 2008 / 2020 / 2022?
- Where will your income come from when you stop working?
- What do you already own, and why did you buy it?
- Who else is involved in decisions (spouse, partner, parents)?
- What has stopped you from doing this before?

## Typical objections

- "I'll do it myself" → fine; offer the plan as a map they can execute alone.
- "Banks give me this for free" → compare what the bank's offer actually includes and who pays the adviser there.
- "Not now, markets are high/low" → the plan does not depend on timing; time in the market beats timing.
- "Need to discuss with spouse" → invite the spouse to the next meeting.
