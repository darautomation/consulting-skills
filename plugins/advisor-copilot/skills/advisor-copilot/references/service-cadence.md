# Service year: meeting cadence and annual review

A year of regular meetings beats a one-time plan: clients stay invested, implement more and refer friends. A cadence that works well: **13 meetings a year**, roughly monthly plus a closing review.

| # | Meeting | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kickoff: goals and data | data checklist complete |
| 2 | Plan presentation | plan agreed |
| 3 | Safety: emergency fund, insurance | gaps closed or scheduled |
| 4 | Debts and cashflow | contribution plan running |
| 5 | Protection bucket implemented | accounts open |
| 6 | Income bucket implemented | |
| 7 | Growth bucket implemented | |
| 8 | Taxes and asset location | structure checked |
| 9 | Family: will, beneficiaries, children | documents in place |
| 10 | Mid-year check | drift vs plan |
| 11 | Life changes, new goals | plan updated |
| 12 | Behaviour check: what the client did in volatility | lessons |
| 13 | Annual review | next year agreed |

Adapt to your service; keep the principle: every meeting has one outcome.

## Tracking

After each meeting update: meeting number, outcome done/not, promised by advisor, promised by client. Update counters only from confirmed facts (recap sent, client confirmed).

## Annual review (meeting 13 or before renewal)

Use `templates/annual-review.md`. Show: where the client started, what changed in numbers (net worth, surplus, contributions made, goals funded %), what we did together, what is left, what next year looks like. Ask for feedback and, only after a visible result, for a referral or testimonial with written permission.

## Risk signals between meetings

- Silence over 14 days after a promised action.
- Two meetings rescheduled in a row.
- Client mentions another adviser or product pitch.
- Big life event (job change, move, child, inheritance, divorce).
Each one → a short check-in draft for the advisor.
