# Financial plan draft: structure

Deliver as a document the advisor can present in one meeting. Client-facing parts in plain language; internal notes clearly separated.

1. **Family and context**: who, ages, residency, currencies, life plans. Goals at 1, 3 and 10 years.
2. **Balance sheet and cashflow**: assets, liabilities, income, expenses, monthly surplus. Only from data; every gap is a question.
3. **Safety first**: emergency fund (months of expenses), insurance gaps (life, health, property, liability), debts to clear.
4. **Three buckets**:
   - *Protection*: capital that must not drop (cash, short bonds, deposits, guaranteed products). Covers 1-3 years of goals.
   - *Income*: assets that pay (bonds, dividend funds, rental property).
   - *Growth*: long-term equity and alternatives, money not needed for 7+ years.
   Give % per bucket and the reason in one line each. Tie each bucket to a goal.
5. **Asset location**: where each bucket lives (bank, broker, insurance wrapper, property, jurisdiction) and why: tax, access, protection, diversification of country and currency risk.
6. **Contributions plan**: how much per month into which bucket; what happens to bonuses and windfalls.
7. **Scenarios**: base / cautious / optimistic with stated assumptions (return range, inflation, currency). No point forecasts.
8. **Next 3 meetings**: concrete agenda for each.
9. **Internal (not for client)**: open questions, risks, product ideas to check, compliance notes.

Done when the advisor can run the next meeting from this document without asking the client basics again.
