# Compliance checklist (generic, not legal advice)

- Personalized investment advice requires a licence in many jurisdictions (e.g., FCA in UK, SEC/FINRA in US, MiFID II in EU, local regulators elsewhere). If the advisor is not licensed for the client's jurisdiction, recaps and plans are education and planning, not product recommendations. Say so in the disclaimer (config `disclaimer`).
- No guarantees, no "risk-free", no past returns presented as future returns.
- Fees and commissions disclosed as the advisor's rules require.
- Suitability: record the client's risk profile, experience and objectives before any product discussion.
- Client data: passports, account numbers, tax IDs never in messengers or AI chats. Use your secure storage.
- Marketing: testimonials only with written consent and as local rules allow.
